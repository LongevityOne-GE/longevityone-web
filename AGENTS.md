# Content Rules

## No Em Dashes
NEVER use em dashes (—) anywhere: not in code, comments, strings, Sanity content, or any text written to files or the browser. Always use a regular hyphen (-) instead. This applies to all languages (English and Georgian).

## No Trailing Punctuation on Headlines
NEVER end a heading, headline, eyebrow, label, button, or CTA with a period, full-stop, or other terminal punctuation (`.`, `!`, `?`). This applies to all `<h1>`-`<h6>`, hero headlines, section titles, card titles, button labels, eyebrows, and Sanity heading fields, in any language (English and Georgian). Body paragraphs and multi-sentence subtext MAY use normal sentence punctuation. Example: write `Your longevity team`, not `Your longevity team.`.
